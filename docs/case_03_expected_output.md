### Question for the board

**Optimal sequencing of locoregional and systemic therapy in a partial responder with KRAS G12C MSS metastatic colorectal cancer — specifically resectability of liver and primary disease, role of consolidation with KRAS G12C-targeted combinations on trial, pre-operative bevacizumab washout, and post-operative therapy duration.**

### Considerations

- *Guideline-aligned:* NCCN recognizes hepatic resection as a potentially curative option in selected oligometastatic colorectal cancer patients with response to systemic therapy; multidisciplinary surgical assessment is the standard next step for this profile
- *Trial pointer:* KRAS G12C-directed combinations (with anti-EGFR, with chemotherapy, or with checkpoint inhibitors) are an active research area in MSS colorectal cancer; trial enrollment may be considered as consolidation if upfront resection is not pursued
- *Workup gap:* the board may want hepatobiliary surgery to formally assess future-liver-remnant adequacy and synchronicity of primary resection, and to confirm the standard 4–6 week interval off bevacizumab pre-operatively given the partial response on biologic therapy
