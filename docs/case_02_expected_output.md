### Question for the board

### Considerations

- *Guideline-aligned:* NCCN supports biopsy confirmation of suspected metastatic recurrence whenever feasible, both to confirm disease and to re-characterize biology, given that receptor status and HER2 expression can shift between primary and metastatic sites
- *Trial pointer:* if metastatic disease is confirmed and endocrine therapy is selected as first-line, ongoing trials are evaluating CDK4/6 inhibitor sequencing and novel oral SERDs in ESR1-mutant disease — molecular profiling on the metastatic specimen would inform eligibility
- *Workup gap:* the CA 27.29 trend (rise from baseline starting Jan 2026, before the imaging finding) supports an active workup; the board may want to address whether a tissue diagnosis or short-interval (8–12 week) repeat imaging is the more appropriate next step given the small nodule size and modest FDG uptake
